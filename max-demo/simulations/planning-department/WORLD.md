## 1. Company (public facts)
- **Thirdwave Corporation (株式会社サードウェーブ)**, private, HQ in **Akihabara**, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo. Founded 1984 as
  a PC parts shop; about 1,100 employees.
- Brands: **GALLERIA** (gaming; No.1 share of Japan's gaming desktops; 2025 relaunch into **X / F / S / GSL / E**
  series), **raytrek** (enterprise, creator and AI workstations, incl. local-LLM bundles), **THIRDWAVE** (value and
  business PCs; **THIRDWAVE-G** budget gaming). Retail: **Dospara** stores (43) + dospara.co.jp, **Digital Dock**
  repair shops (15+), **GALLERIA Lounge** showroom in Akihabara.
- Sites (Kanagawa): **Ayase factory** (BTO assembly + logistics hub), **Ebina office** (development + support),
  **Hiratsuka** (logistics + call centre). Promise: ships **within 2 days** of order (normal times).
- Fiscal year **August–July**. This year = **FY2027/7 (44th term), 1 Aug 2026 – 31 Jul 2027**.
  Q1 = Aug–Oct, **Q2 = Nov–Jan**, Q3 = Feb–Apr, Q4 = May–Jul. **H1 closes 31 Jan 2027** (半期決算).
  Customers in government/education/enterprise budget April–March (their spending peaks Feb–Mar).
- Revenue history (public): FY2024/7 ¥77.1B → FY2025/7 ¥92.6B (Windows 10 replacement year).
  FY2026/7 (simulation value): **¥101B**.

## 2. Market context, Nov 2026 (public facts)
- **Windows 10 hangover**: support ended 14 Oct 2025; Japan PC shipments Apr–Sep 2026 **−33.5%** YoY (desktops
  −37.1%) while the average price rose **+40.3%** (JEITA).
- **Memory shock**: AI/HBM demand pulled DRAM/NAND capacity away from PCs. Dospara's 32GB×2 DDR5 kit went from
  about ¥16,000 (Sep 2025) to **¥119,800 (2 Feb 2026)**. In Dec 2025 several BTO makers paused orders.
  **Dospara cancelled the 2026 New Year lucky bags (announced 31 Dec 2025).** TrendForce: PC DRAM contract prices
  **+13–18% in Oct–Dec 2026**; Korean suppliers plan to **cut PC DRAM output by more than 20% in 2027**.
- GPUs: RTX 50 series is the mainstream; **RTX 50 SUPER delayed indefinitely**. AMD RX 9070 XT / 9060 XT in GALLERIA F.
- CPUs: Intel Core Ultra 200S and AMD Ryzen 9000/X3D now. **Intel Nova Lake (Core Ultra 400) and AMD Zen 6**
  expected to be revealed at **CES (early Jan 2027)**, shipping Q1–Q2 2027.
- Yen: about **¥158/USD** spot (budget rate ¥156). A ¥5 move ≈ 3% on parts cost.
- Competitors: Mouse Computer (G-Tune; parent MCJ went private Jun 2026), TSUKUMO (G-GEAR), FRONTIER (ran New Year
  "福箱" lucky boxes in Jan 2026), Sycom; Dell/HP/Lenovo hold prices longer thanks to bigger memory contracts.
- Dospara **Black Friday 2026: 13 Nov 11:00 – 4 Dec 10:59** (coupons up to ¥100k + points). The early-bird
  customisation sale (30 Oct–13 Nov) and Black Friday offer the **32GB memory upgrade for +¥9,696**.

## 4. Numbers (fictional, consistent everywhere; ¥B = billion yen, ¥M = million yen)

- Gross margin **17.6% vs 19.0%** (memory cost). By brand (revenue): GALLERIA ¥14.1B, THIRDWAVE ¥4.6B,

### Memory (Daniel + Sarah)
- Q4 (Oct–Dec) DRAM contract: **+15%** vs Q3. Current DRAM coverage: **7 weeks**.
- **Supplier quote for Q1 2027 (Jan–Mar): 120,000 × 16GB DDR5 DIMMs at +14% ≈ ¥1.4B (≈ ¥11,700 each)**, signature
  needed by **4 Dec 2026**; supplier warns of **allocation cuts in 2027** (PC DRAM output −20%+).
- Options: (a) commit 100% (¥1.4B), (b) **commit 70% = 84,000 DIMMs ≈ ¥0.98B** + buy the rest spot/monthly,

### Pricing (Jacob)

- End the **+¥9,696 32GB upgrade** when Black Friday ends (4 Dec); new 32GB upgrade price **+¥19,800**.
- Competitors already raised (FRONTIER +16–41% in Dec 2025; MCJ passed costs through).

### GPUs and the enterprise tender (Daniel + Tom)
